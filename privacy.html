Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

The Build Room publishes short video clips on our own channels on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. This policy explains what data our publishing tool handles.

Who we are

The Build Room is run by Pier Paolo Corteggiani. Contact: corteggianipierpaolo+buildroom@gmail.com.

Our publishing tool and Google user data

Our internal tool ("Build Room Clips") uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API only to upload videos to, and read statistics of, our own YouTube channel @buildroomclips. It is used only by us, on our own account.

  • Data accessed: the channel's own videos, upload status and view statistics.
  • Use: to publish our clips and to decide the best publishing times from view counts.
  • Storage: the access token is stored only on our own computer, in a file readable only by its owner. Statistics are kept in local files.
  • Sharing: we do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. We do not use it for advertising.
  • Deletion: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; on revocation we delete the stored token.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We also follow the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Visitors and viewers

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. When you watch our clips, the platform you use (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook) handles your data under its own privacy policy; we only see the aggregate statistics those platforms give to creators.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.
